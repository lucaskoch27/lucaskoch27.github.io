About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Statistics at Penn State University, with research interests in computational social science and causal inference. My passion lies in working at the intersection of statistics and social-political issues, with the broader goal of developing novel statistical methodologies to understand complex societal challenges.

Prior to joining Penn State, I graduated from Saint Louis University with a B.S in Data Science and Mathematics in 2026, conducting research with the PRiME Center analyzing education trends in Missouri, and Dr. Haijun Gong, focusing using statistical tools to analyze gene regulatory networks.
