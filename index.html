About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Statistics at Penn State University. My research interests are in applied and methodological questions for the social sciences, with an emphasis on public policy, political and social inequality, and spatially structured disparities. particularly interested in questions involving race, education, urban-rural differences, elections, and political institutions. Methodologically, my interests lie in causal inference, Bayesian statistics, and the analysis of complex social and administrative data. Prior to joining Penn State, I graduated from Saint Louis University with a B.S in Data Science and Mathematics in 2026, conducting research with the PRiME Center doing Education Policy Analysis, and Dr. Haijun Gong, focusing using statistical tools to analyze Gene Regulatory Networks.
